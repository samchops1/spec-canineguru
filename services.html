Rates & Services

I come to you.

All services are on location — in your home or at the park. Prices below are copied from the current Rates & Services page. Payment is due once the consultation is concluded and/or the lesson begins.

Consultation

$65

Need a professional opinion? Not sure how many lessons you will need? Up to 30 minutes. The consultation fee is waived if you sign up for lessons during the visit — you pay the lesson or package instead. If you buy a package, the consultation becomes part of the first lesson and we start right away.

Private lesson at the park

$85

Pay as you go. One private lesson at the park, weather permitting — for people who prefer I not come to the house. Good for socializing, behavior modification, walking, searching, hunting, AKC CGC training, and the like. Homework between lessons. I teach you how to teach, and communicate with, your dog.

Private lesson in the home

$95

Pay as you go. One private lesson in your home. Behavior problems, obedience, AKC CGC training, crate training, potty training. I work with you and your dog together. Homework between lessons.

Alpha Lesson

$565

A consultation and a private lesson in the home that can last up to 4 hours, plus a follow-up visit as needed. Like having your own seminar about your dog(s) in your house: how your dog thinks, why they do what they do, what you can change, and how conditioning builds associations. You get a canine behaviorist for up to 4 hours instead of an hour at a time. For serious behavior issues, or for people who simply want to understand their dogs. Must be scheduled on weekends, and may be subject to a waiting list.

Bundled private lessons

5–10% off

Best value if you pay up front. Discount depends on how many lessons you buy at once. In-home or park, your choice. Contact Brandon for the exact bundle that fits.

Other services

Nail trim

$20

Per dog. Price varies with difficulty. Clippers or dremel. For difficult dogs that groomers may refuse.

Walking

$30

Per dog. Fuel charge may apply. Up to a 2-hour walk, waste pickup during the walk, and leash training as needed. Includes transport to a nearby park.

In-home bath

$45

Base price. Drying, brushing, and nail trim. You provide shampoo and the tub. For dogs that hate the tub or are afraid of cars.

Puppy Finder program

$75

Help picking the right dog. I come to your home, look at how you live, and match temperament and energy — including a companion for a dog you already have. I call breeders and shelters, inquire, and arrange meetings. Shelter and breeder fees are not included. I do not support puppy mills.
