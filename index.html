All services are on location — in your home or at the park. I teach you how to teach, and communicate with, your dog. That is how the relationship actually changes.

Brandon’s story

The dogs I am usually called for

I am a specialist in the dog training community. I work primarily with dogs that are aggressive, fearful, or have an obsessive behavior of some kind.

Aggressive

I use canine language to predict a dog’s next move — and to help prevent unwanted behaviors and fights. Then I teach you those skills, in a way that makes sense.

Fearful

We learn their language. That is how trust gets built, and how a fearful dog can finally understand the rules without being shouted at in ours.

Obsessive

I use positive methods to teach new behaviors, and canine language to redirect the ones that have a hold on the house. Patience brings peace. Frustration leads to chaos.
